Privacy Policy

Anchor — Recovery Companion · Last updated June 13, 2026

In short: Anchor does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal data. Everything you enter — check-ins, journal entries, triggers, and settings — is stored only on your own device. There is no account, no server, and no tracking.

What information Anchor stores

Anchor stores the following information directly on your device (using local app storage):

  • Daily check-in entries (mood, HALT ratings, urges, and related notes)
  • Journal entries you write
  • Custom triggers you create
  • Streak and progress history
  • App preferences, such as theme, daily reminder time, and whether App Lock is enabled

None of this information is sent to us or to any third party. We have no servers that store or process your data, and we cannot see, access, or recover it.

App Lock (PIN) and biometric unlock

If you enable App Lock, your PIN is stored on your device only as a salted, one-way hash — the PIN itself is never saved or transmitted. If you enable biometric unlock (fingerprint or face recognition), Anchor uses your device's built-in biometric system to verify your identity. Anchor never receives, stores, or has access to your biometric data — that is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

Data export and backup

Anchor lets you export your data to a file so you can keep your own backup. That file is saved wherever you choose (e.g., your device storage or a cloud service you select) and is never sent to us.

Daily reminders and notifications

If you enable daily check-in reminders, the reminder is scheduled and triggered entirely on your device. No data is sent anywhere to make this work.

Crisis support resources

Anchor's Crisis Support screen provides quick links to call or text outside support services, such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, the SAMHSA National Helpline, and the Crisis Text Line. Tapping these links opens your device's phone or messaging app to contact these services directly. Anchor does not relay, log, or store anything about these interactions. Any information you share with those services is governed by their own privacy practices, not Anchor's.

Third-party resources used to display the app

To render its interface, Anchor loads a small number of standard open-source web resources (fonts and icon/styling libraries) from public content delivery networks. Loading these resources may expose basic technical information (such as your IP address) to those providers as part of normal web requests, in the same way any website or app does when loading a font or image. Anchor itself does not share any of your recovery data, check-ins, or journal entries with these or any other providers.

Children's privacy

Anchor is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — because Anchor does not collect personal information at all.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you are always in full control. You can delete individual entries within the app, or remove all data at once by uninstalling Anchor or clearing its app storage from your device settings. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered by us, because we never had a copy.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continuing to use Anchor after a change means you accept the updated policy.
