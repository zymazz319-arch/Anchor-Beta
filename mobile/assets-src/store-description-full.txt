Anchor is a private, judgment-free companion for your recovery journey — built around the simple idea that small daily check-ins add up to real change.

Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking — your data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it.

DAILY CHECK-INS
Take a moment each day to check in with yourself using the HALT method (Hunger, Anger, Loneliness, Tired), plus an overall mood rating and urge/compulsion level. Building this simple habit helps you notice patterns before they become problems.

STREAKS THAT MATTER
Track your current streak, longest streak, and total check-ins — gentle motivation to keep showing up for yourself, one day at a time.

TRIGGERS & PATTERNS
Log what triggers difficult moments — stress, loneliness, boredom, social pressure, and more — and see which ones come up most often.

INSIGHTS
Visualize your mood trends, average HALT levels, and urge intensity over time with simple, easy-to-read charts that help you understand your own rhythms.

JOURNAL & REFLECTION
Guided prompts make it easy to reflect on your day, celebrate small wins, and process hard moments — all in a private space that's just for you.

CRISIS SUPPORT, ALWAYS ONE TAP AWAY
Quick access to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, the SAMHSA National Helpline, and the Crisis Text Line — available even when App Lock is enabled.

APP LOCK
Add a PIN or biometric lock so your check-ins and journal stay private, even if someone else picks up your phone.

DAILY REMINDERS
Optional gentle reminders help you build a consistent check-in habit.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
Export your data anytime for backup or to share with a therapist or sponsor, and import it back whenever you need.

Anchor is a self-help tool and is not a substitute for professional treatment. If you're in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
